Privacy Policy for KidCost

Last updated: October 8, 2026

KidCost is a child expense tracking application designed to help parents and guardians record, organize and understand expenses related to their children. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Information We Collect

KidCost may collect information that you voluntarily provide while using the application, such as child names, expense amounts, expense categories, dates, notes and other information entered by you.

If account or cloud services are enabled, information may also be processed through third-party services such as Firebase.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Record and organize child-related expenses.
  • Calculate spending totals and reports.
  • Display expense history and statistics.
  • Provide and improve the application.
  • Maintain application security and functionality.

Data Storage

Depending on the version and features of the application, expense information may be stored locally on your device and/or using cloud services.

Third-Party Services

KidCost may use third-party services such as Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, analytics, crash reporting or other application functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

KidCost is intended for parents and guardians. Users should not enter unnecessary sensitive information about children into the application. KidCost does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for the purpose of creating accounts.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Data Deletion

Users may delete information stored within the application using available delete features. If cloud account services are provided, users may request deletion of their account and associated information by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
